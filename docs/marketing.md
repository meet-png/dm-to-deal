## Format research (why these work)

- **X/Twitter:** the hook is 80–90% of performance; front-load the payoff in
  the first ~8 words. Best formats: "specific outcome + mystery", "I built X".
  Thread sweet spot ≈ 7 tweets.
- **LinkedIn:** the first ~210 characters show before "see more" — the hook
  must land there. Algorithm rewards dwell time + comments + saves; the first
  60 minutes decide reach. Best openers: bold/contrarian statement, surprising
  stat, or a high-stakes problem.
- **Niche pattern:** "soft-launch + curiosity gap + comment-a-keyword" — it
  self-selects warm leads into your DMs (and mirrors what the product does).
